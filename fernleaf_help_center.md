# Fernleaf Help Center (sample document)

A short sample document for trying the "add documents" and Suggest fix features. Fernleaf Software is a made-up company.

## Mobile apps
Fernleaf has free mobile apps for iPhone (iOS 16 or later) and Android (Android 11 or later). The apps support tasks, comments, time tracking and approvals; invoicing and reports are web only.

## Free trial
Every new workspace gets a 14-day free trial of the Business plan, with no credit card needed. When the trial ends, the workspace moves to the free Starter plan unless you choose a paid plan, and no data is deleted.

## Customer support hours
Support is available by chat and email at support@fernleaf.example, Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 8 PM Central Time. Business customers get priority support with a first reply within 4 hours.

## Integrations
Fernleaf connects with Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, GitHub, GitLab, QuickBooks, Xero, Stripe and Zapier. An admin sets them up from Settings, then Integrations.

## Data export and backups
Admins can export all workspace data as CSV and JSON from Settings, then Export. Fernleaf backs up all data every hour and keeps backups for 35 days.
